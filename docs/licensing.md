HASLAB uses open-source reciprocal licenses. This follows the owner's explicit
choice to allow commercial use with applicable reciprocal sharing obligations,
rather than require permission/payment for commercial use.

| Material | License | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Original hardware designs and design documentation | CERN-OHL-S-2.0 | Strong reciprocal licensing designed for hardware source and products |
| Original software, including future compiler/runtime/firmware | GPL-3.0-or-later | Copyleft for covered software and derivative distributions |
| Third-party IP, tools, models, and datasets | Their original licenses | No grant or relicensing by HASLAB |

The binding scope is in [LICENSE](../LICENSE); full texts are in
[LICENSES](../LICENSES/).

Both licenses allow commercial use. They do not make every commercial user ask
permission, pay a fee, or publish all private experiments. GPL obligations are
principally tied to conveying covered software; it is not AGPL's network-use
source provision. CERN-OHL-S has its own conditions for conveying covered
source and products, including access to the applicable complete source. Read
the actual license for the scope and exceptions. See the official
[CERN hardware license](https://ohwr.org/licences/) and
[GNU GPL](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html).

Zubin Bhuyan can use the project commercially under these licenses, as can
other users. As copyright holder, Zubin can also offer different terms for
material for which he holds sufficient rights. Contributions from others do
not automatically confer a right to relicense their work under proprietary
terms; that requires appropriate permission or a separate contribution
agreement. No copyright assignment or broad relicensing agreement is introduced
here.

If separate proprietary commercial licensing becomes a goal, settle the
contribution-rights policy before accepting contributions on that basis. A
reciprocal license alone is not an exclusive monetization right. A review by
counsel familiar with hardware licensing is appropriate before relying on a
commercial licensing program.

Use file-level SPDX identifiers on future sources. Keep hardware and software
license boundaries explicit, and retain third-party notices. Model exports,
weights, and dependencies must be reviewed under their own licenses before
redistribution; HASLAB's license cannot override them.
